# Contributing to Metis

Thank you for contributing to Metis. By participating, you agree to follow the
licensing terms below.

## Contribution licence

By submitting a contribution for inclusion in this repository, you agree to
provide it under the Apache License, Version 2.0, without additional terms or
conditions. See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). If you cannot grant those rights, do not
submit the contribution.

You must have the right and authority to submit your contribution. Do not
submit confidential information or material that you do not have permission to
license. Identify any third-party code, documentation, data, or media and
preserve its applicable notices.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

Sign off each commit to certify the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/). Add
the following line to the commit message using your real name and an email
address that you control:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

Git can add the sign-off automatically when you create a commit with
`git commit --signoff`.

## Project names and branding

Contributions do not receive permission to use the Metis project names or
branding except as allowed by applicable law and [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).
